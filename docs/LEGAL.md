# Legal surface

The product generates letters that look like legal / medical documents
and sends them to health insurers. That creates exposure if users treat
the output as professional advice. This document describes the
disclaimers and UI gates that mitigate the exposure.

> **Draft for legal review.** Wording below is a starting point. An
> attorney licensed in your operating jurisdictions must finalize the
> actual disclaimers, Terms of Service, and Acceptable Use Policy before
> launch.

---

## What the system does that carries risk

1. **Generates appeal letters**. Output includes clinical language and
   references to "standards of care." Users could submit generated text
   without review.
2. **Extracts patient data from uploaded documents**. OCR + Claude. The
   system is probabilistic; the post-validation drops fabricated codes
   but cannot guarantee correctness of all extracted fields.
3. **Persists PHI**. Storage failure modes affect patient care workflows.

---

## Required disclaimer (draft)

Show this on every generated appeal preview. Users must acknowledge before
export / submission.

> **Review required before submission.** This draft was generated by an
> AI system using the denial letter and patient context you provided. It
> is **not** legal, medical, or clinical advice. You (or a licensed
> professional under your direction) must verify every factual claim,
> procedure code, diagnosis code, date, and identifier before submitting
> this appeal to any payer. The generator may omit details, misquote
> policy, or produce language that is not appropriate for your specific
> patient's situation. By exporting or submitting this draft, you accept
> sole responsibility for its contents.

### UI requirements

- A banner on the appeal preview (`AppealPreview.tsx`) that contains the
  above language.
- A mandatory acknowledgment checkbox — user must tick "I have reviewed
  this draft and accept responsibility for its contents" before the
  **Download** or **Copy** action is enabled.
- Record the acknowledgment event in the audit log
  (`action=appeal.export`, `metadata={ acknowledged: true }`).

These are not yet wired in the frontend; track as a launch blocker.

## Terms of Service — points to cover

Work with counsel on the actual document. Topics that are non-obvious
for this product:

- **Scope of use.** Prior Auth Assistant is a drafting and workflow tool
  for licensed healthcare workers. It is not a substitute for clinical
  or legal judgment and is not intended for direct patient-facing use.
- **No attorney-client relationship.** Using the product does not create
  such a relationship with the vendor or its employees.
- **Accuracy disclaimer.** AI-generated content may contain errors;
  user is responsible for verification.
- **PHI handling.** Reference the BAA. Customer retains ownership of PHI;
  vendor processes it on behalf of the customer.
- **Prohibited uses.** No training on customer PHI without explicit
  opt-in. No use for discriminatory underwriting, insurance-fraud
  investigation, etc.
- **Uptime / availability.** An SLO; not a guarantee.
- **Indemnity & limitation of liability.** Standard carve-outs; counsel's
  call.
- **Data retention and deletion on termination.** Align with
  `docs/COMPLIANCE.md` retention table.

## Acceptable Use Policy — points to cover

- No uploading records for patients outside the user's scope of practice.
- No using the system to generate appeals for patients the organization
  has no relationship with.
- No circumventing rate limits or per-org quotas.
- No reverse engineering the models or prompt structure.
- Reporting security issues through a defined channel.

---

## Model output auditing

Consider logging a cryptographic hash of the generated appeal letter
against the request, so the organization can prove what was returned by
the system at a given time. Useful for disputes with customers about
what the AI "said."

Implementation sketch: SHA-256 of `letter_content` stored in a new
indexed column on `AppealRecord`, plus the `audit_log` entry for
`appeal.create` already includes the `resource_id`. Not yet implemented;
track as a post-launch improvement.

## Jurisdictions

Healthcare regulations vary by state (record retention, consent,
breach-notification windows). The product does not currently
jurisdiction-aware; operators deploy a single instance per customer.

If you plan to offer this as a multi-tenant SaaS across states, counsel
will need to opine on whether per-state configuration (retention,
notification) is required. Defer until that market exists.
